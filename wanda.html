Her Story

Born in Sokovia alongside her twin brother Pietro, Wanda Maximoff grew from an unlikely Avenger into one of the most formidable mystic figures in existence. Shaped by loss, devotion, and ancient occult lore, her connection to Chaos Magic defines her destiny as the Scarlet Witch—a force capable of unmaking or reshaping worlds when pushed to her limit.

Myth & Mantle

Wanda & The Scarlet Witch

Long before she ever wore the crown, the name "Scarlet Witch" whispered through mystical prophecy as a rare, untamed force. When Wanda finally embraced who she truly was, the lines between grief, love, and cosmic creation blurred—turning a quiet girl trying to belong into a living legend of magic.
