Her Story

Existing since the dawn of life and mortality, Rio Vidal walks the threshold between the living world and the afterlife. As the first Green Witch and the personification of Death, her presence is both terrifying and strangely magnetic—bound by timeless rituals, dark devotion, and an unbreakable cosmic duty.

Myth & Mantle

Rio Vidal & Lady Death

Behind her sharp wit and quiet authority lies the oldest truth of all: everything returns to the earth. Whether tending wild roots or claiming what is owed, Rio reminds everyone she crosses paths with that endings are simply part of the design.
