Her Story

Born over ten thousand years ago, Selene has shaped empires, secret societies, and mutant politics from the shadows. Cold, regal, and endlessly calculating, she treats immortality and power as a birthright worth any sacrifice.

Myth & Mantle

The Black Queen's Crown

Selene never waits for power to be handed to her—she drains it from rivals, bends ancient blood magic to her will, and outlasts enemies who thought they had the upper hand.
